\section*{Affirmation}
\vspace{-2ex}\titlerule\vspace{4ex}

This master degree report, \textit{Automation of Pyrometer Data Pre-processing for Metal Forming and Heat Treatment}, was written as part of the master degree work needed to obtain a Master of Engineering Science with specialization in AI and Automation degree at University West. All material in this report that is not my own is clearly identified and used in an appropriate and correct way. The main part of the work included in this degree project has not previously been published or used for obtaining another degree.

This thesis represents my individual contribution to a joint project. The sensor calibration stage~(ATP-2), including the implementation and evaluation of mean offset correction, linear regression, polynomial regression, piecewise linear calibration, Random Forest regression, MLP neural network, Gradient Boosting, and Support Vector Regression, constitutes my individual work. The additional compression methods~(Delta Encoding, Variational Autoencoder, and Deep Autoencoder) also constitute my individual contribution to the compression stage~(ATP-3). The signal denoising stage~(ATP-1) and the classical and PCA-based compression methods are addressed in a separate individual thesis by Mallepalli Sravya Reddy~\cite{sravya2026}.
